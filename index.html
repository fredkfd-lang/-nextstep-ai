🔐 Privacy first

Your profile is stored locally in encrypted form on this device in this first version. Nothing is submitted to an external application website. You can export or delete your data at any time.

Simple Datenschutz: BerOpp uses your profile only for the features you choose. We do not automatically submit applications. You can edit, export or delete your data.
